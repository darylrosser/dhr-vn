How To Get In Touch

First, don't try social media. I have profiles. I don't check them.

Second, who should reach out?

I'm always up for nerdy conversations with people who think way too hard about things that absolutely do not matter. And possibly sometimes things that do. If you're into sci-fi, business biographies, creating absolutely pointless things because "fun", or you just want to chat about something I've written - send me a message.

Fair warning: I'm not interested in "opportunities" that involve me giving you money, access to my customer list, or promoting your thing. If that's why you're here, we're probably not a match.

But if you're a fellow nerd, you've got a genuine question, or you just want to argue about which Cixin Liu novel is best (it's The Dark Forest, obviously), my inbox is open.

If that sounds like you, use the form below or find my email address - it's literally my first name at my surname.com (minus the H part).

Besides writing nonsense on this website, I do run actual companies, read far too many books, and am getting excruciatingly close to having a wedding to the point where every other hour is some sort of planning activity.

So I may not reply. But if you're a real person, trying to have a conversation, not sell or ask me for something, I'll try my best.
